# EMBER & OAK — MEDIA & PHOTOGRAPHY ART DIRECTION

Status: **VISUAL PRODUCTION BRIEF**

## 1. Photography role

Photography is a primary design material.

The visual system should communicate:
- fire.
- texture.
- ingredient quality.
- craft.
- intimacy.
- evening atmosphere.

## 2. Image characteristics

Preferred:
- directional warm light.
- deep shadows with retained detail.
- tactile textures.
- restrained saturation.
- natural plating.
- real service moments.
- strong negative space for editorial layouts.

Avoid:
- bright food-delivery photography.
- generic stock smiles.
- oversaturated orange filters.
- artificial neon lighting unless authentic to venue.
- flat overhead catalog shots as the dominant style.

## 3. Hero

Primary:
- portrait signature dish.
- high visual contrast.
- clear subject.
- enough surrounding negative space for responsive crop.

Optional secondary:
- fire/kitchen/ingredient detail.

## 4. Philosophy

Choose:
- hands/chef process.
- raw ingredient.
- flame/fire.
- supplier detail where rights exist.

## 5. Signature menu images

Need:
- consistent grading.
- unique composition per dish.
- image still readable at smaller side panel sizes.

## 6. Atmosphere

Strong candidates:
- dining room in evening service.
- warm table lighting.
- chef/open kitchen.
- wine pour/service.

Media should sell the evening, not just interior architecture.

## 7. Chef

Portrait:
- confident but natural.
- editorial.
- not corporate headshot.

Working image:
- real kitchen action.
- respectful of background clutter.

## 8. Private Dining

Each experience needs a distinct image:
- Private Room.
- Chef's Table.
- Full Buyout.

The image should communicate capacity and spatial mood, not only table decoration.

## 9. Gallery

Mix:
- close-up food.
- human craft.
- ingredients.
- architecture.
- service.
- guest experience with rights.

Avoid gallery consisting entirely of plates.

## 10. Crop families

Recommended source masters should support:

```text
portrait      4:5 / 3:4
landscape     3:2 / 16:9
editorial     2:3
square        1:1 only when useful
```

Do not crop all assets into identical ratios.

## 11. Overlay text safety

When text overlays media:
- select image with negative space, or
- use controlled dark overlay.

Do not cover the primary subject with long copy.

## 12. Video

If used:
- short ambient sequence.
- no dialogue required unless captioned.
- muted.
- poster image.
- no large autoplay mobile asset.

## 13. Color grading

Aim:
- warm highlights.
- neutral food color accuracy.
- charcoal shadows.
- avoid crushing all black detail.
- accent orange should come from fire/brand details naturally rather than heavy global tint.

## 14. Rights

Do not display:
- guest faces without appropriate rights.
- award/Michelin imagery without verified entitlement.
- third-party supplier photography without usage permission.

## 15. Asset priority for Phase 4 high-fidelity comps

P0 visual blockers:

```text
brand wordmark
home hero dish
philosophy/fire image
featured dish images
atmosphere image
chef portrait
private dining images
```

If final assets are unavailable, high-fidelity comps may use clearly labeled art-direction placeholders but cannot be treated as final production creative.
